# Transformation

## Role

Transformation is the operative threshold of Exocortex.

It is the process through which captured material stops being only stored information and begins to generate structure.

Transformation does not mean summarizing material.
It means detecting relations, tensions, recurrences, shifts, and possible forms inside accumulated material.

## Position in the architecture

capture → memory → transformation → structure → action

Transformation is the passage between memory and structure.

Memory keeps material available.
Transformation makes this material active.
Structure stabilizes the forms that emerge from transformation.

## What transformation does

Transformation may include:

- segmentation
- extraction
- relation detection
- recurrence detection
- clustering
- contrast detection
- attractor formation
- consolidation into stable structures

## Inputs

Transformation can operate on:

- notes
- logs
- images
- transcripts
- documents
- domain material
- conceptual fragments
- structured corpora

## Outputs

Transformation may produce:

- units
- relations
- patterns
- invariants
- tensions
- clusters
- attractors
- paths
- structural candidates

## Principle

The purpose of transformation is not to reduce material.

Its purpose is to reveal the internal architecture that begins to appear when material accumulates, repeats, diverges, or reorganizes itself.

## Relation to the Cortex Layer Engine

The Cortex Layer Engine may later become one technical or formal implementation of transformation.

At this stage, transformation remains a core primitive.

The engine must not replace the primitive.
It may instantiate it.
